# Coherent asset families — unvalidated

**Status: unvalidated.** Source-production guidance, not a generator or approval to
redesign art. Follow the main skill and applicable construction modules. Use for related
props, materials, sprites, icons or UI-art states; in-game verification remains with
`gummy-game-verification` after integration is approved.

## 1. Establish the family's contract

Inspect existing game/source assets before choosing a style. Record camera/view, native
display size or closest 3D distance, shape language, palette roles, value contrast, edge
treatment, material response, detail density, scale, pivots and animation character.
Use observable properties and approved references, not an aesthetic label alone.
Do not replace the existing direction without approval.

List required members, variations and states in the existing production manifest or
approved brief. Distinguish placeholders, candidate sources and approved delivery.
Record provenance and rights per input. Reuse current manifest/catalog contracts,
not a competing runtime registry; family approval does not license source artwork.

## 2. Approve one representative target

Choose a member exercising the hardest material, silhouette and background contrast.
Build/source only it first. For 3D pass the construction gate before material polish;
for icons/sprites inspect native size on actual gameplay backgrounds. An enlarged
hero image is not a readability test.

Before batching, obtain required style/shape acceptance with named views and explicit
locks. Fix a failed target before producing siblings. A scope change must identify
invalidated decisions/evidence rather than silently replacing the approved target.

## 3. Produce controlled variations

Keep palette roles, view/facing, proportions, edges and detail scale stable. Change only
approved distinguishing features. Preserve editable sources and reproducible job/settings
records. Unrelated prompts do not form a coherent family merely because colours match.

Normalize dimensions, framing, pivots, slicing and names deterministically. Preserve
originals and inspect normalized output. Generated materials follow the existing colour/
content gate: do not recolour to conceal stains, seams or incorrect detail. For 3D,
geometry, boundaries, UVs and rigging remain independently verifiable.

## 4. Review the complete family

1. Compare consistent framing plus native-size views. For 3D also compare equal world
   scale so individual framing does not conceal size mismatches.
2. Check silhouette, palette roles, light direction, texture density and material
   roughness/reflectivity. Inspect alpha edges on bright/dark backgrounds.
3. Verify all required states/variants exist and remain distinguishable. Colour alone
   may not communicate a gameplay-critical difference.
4. Inspect animation identity, volume, pivots, timing and frame transitions in playback,
   not just a sheet. Inspect tiles in repeat/offset previews and along their boundaries.
5. After separately approved integration inspect actual cameras, backgrounds, quality
   tiers and material support. A contact sheet does not establish runtime appearance.

## 5. Acceptance and failure response

Record each member's source/version, locks, differences, required files, captures and
pass/fail/unverified status. Repair drift at its source; do not compensate with arbitrary
runtime scaling/tint or a new loader cache without an approved decision. Missing states
are not shippable placeholders unless explicitly approved as such.

File validity, visual cohesion, gameplay readability and user acceptance are separate
claims. A successful family validates only the tested method and conditions.
