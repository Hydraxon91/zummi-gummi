# Source and license

Workflow guidance in `references/asset-workflows.md` is adapted from these skills in
https://github.com/EL4CTEO/blender-skills at commit
`71d031dab8bc6729504d11a78cbe91945401045f` (2026-10-16):

- blender-asset-qa
- blender-hardsurface-modeling
- blender-retopology-uv
- blender-export-pipelines (including references/godot-web.md)
- blender-game-optimization
- blender-pbr-materials
- blender-texture-baking
- blender-geometry-nodes

Local reporting scripts are project-specific implementations of selected checks, not
copies of the upstream scripts. No upstream installer, dependencies, repair scripts,
review camera or compressed-export presets are installed. Upstream claims about test
coverage do not establish validation of these local adaptations.

`references/openings-and-glazing.md` and `scripts/render_state_audit.py` are project-authored from
the Raptor RC glazing round, not adapted from the upstream suite.

`scripts/texture_inventory.py` and the external-review guidance are project-authored;
they are not copied from the upstream suite.
