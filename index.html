Controls

Bears

Drag a bear
Pull and stretch it; release to throw.
Two fingers
Pull two bears — or one bear from both sides.
Pour / Reset
Add five bears, or clear the table to zero.
L / Launch bear
Arm the gummy cannon, aim at the table, then click or tap to fire a bear. Esc cancels.
Move cannon
Drag the cannon, or click Move cannon and then the table. Esc cancels.
Limit
How many bears may exist (8–256).
F / Explode
Firework at the pointer — hold F to spray, Shift+F for a fan; the button volleys three at the pile.
Upright
Bears gently right themselves once settled; pushes still win.
Health & night
Health bars show condition. Hurt or tired bears return to their villa; from 20:00 to 06:00 they sleep there and wake when morning comes. Very hungry bears may raid a beehive and risk a sting.
